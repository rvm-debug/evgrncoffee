// about

coffee shop by day,
experiment by design.

evgr.n is a coffee shop run entirely by students — the baristas, the buyers, the people who chose the beans and painted the menu board. We wanted a place on campus to slow down for ten minutes between classes, and we figured the best way to get one was to build it ourselves.

The lab part is real: we rotate roasters, test new drinks, run cuppings and latte-art nights, and treat every shift as a chance to learn how a small business actually works.
