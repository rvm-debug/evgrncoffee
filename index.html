// about

coffee shop by day,
experiment by design.

evgr.n is a student-run coffee shop in EVGR C. Our goal is to create a unifying community space for grad students and undergrads, right in the heart of EVGR.

Grad students need more places to gather. Undergrads don't spend enough time at EVGR. And right now, there's no coffee shop anywhere in Escondido Village. We want to fix all three with one good cup of coffee.

The lab part means we're always experimenting: new drinks, new ideas, and new ways to bring people together.
